About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a fast, lightweight web application designed to help you organize thoughts and tasks seamlessly. It provides a simple, clutter-free space to record ideas instantly before they escape your memory.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your thought or reminder into the "New note" text input box on the home page.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button or press Enter to save your note to the active list.
  3. Review your saved notes at any time under the "Your notes" section.

Features

  • Instant input field with automatic submission handling.
  • Real-time counter displaying total recorded notes.
  • Accessible semantic structure optimized for keyboard navigation and screen readers.
